VIBEFY
An independent music player by Spinti

Website: https://usevibefy.online
Source and releases: https://github.com/Spinti1/Vibefy

This setup installs Vibefy for your Windows account. Updates preserve your
existing application settings, connected accounts and library data.

After installation, connect Spotify and YouTube in the app. Microsoft Edge
WebView2 Runtime is required for the dedicated YouTube sign-in window.
Service availability and account permissions depend on each provider.

CREDITS
