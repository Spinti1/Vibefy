Optional private YouTube Music copies of your Spotify playlists, with one linked view in Vibefy.

- Connect both services, then enable **Spotify → YouTube Music** in Settings → Connected music accounts. The setting is saved for that account pair.
- Initial imports and recording matching run in the background. Cached matches let linked playlist playback reuse known YouTube IDs. Initial syncing can take time; network-dependent playback is not guaranteed to be instant.
- Matching checks title, artist credits, version and duration. Uncertain recordings appear in a paginated review list with manual choices.
- Song additions to linked playlists are queued independently for Spotify and YouTube Music. Spotify's existing rate-limit outbox remains intact.
- Private copies use recovery markers, durable write intents, acknowledgement checks and occurrence-count reconciliation. Interrupted or uncertain writes stop for review instead of being blindly repeated.
- Source deletions/reordering remain separate. Private copies are not deleted when sync is disabled. Local files/episodes are not transferred.
- Sync runs locally per account pair. Use one syncing installation per pair at a time; cross-device exactly-once synchronization is not guaranteed.

Spotify import still requires working authorization and respects its rate limits. The YouTube Music browser-session interface is unofficial and can change.
