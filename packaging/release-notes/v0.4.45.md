Vibefy 0.4.45 makes visualizers built-in and puts every effect in the main selector.

- Choose directly between 2 Vivy, 2 Backmusic and 12 SoundVisualizer effects.
- Remove the nested style dropdown from Settings. Intensity, brightness, FPS and display settings stay there.
- Visualizers no longer require installing a mod, downloading a package or reloading the app. Existing visualizer settings are preserved.
- The original project credits and license notices remain included.
- Reopening Vibefy remembers the last song, position and playlist, including YouTube/Linked playlist order and repeated songs. Playback stays paused until you press Play.

The Windows installer is unsigned. SmartScreen may display a warning.
