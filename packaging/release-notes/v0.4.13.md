# Vibefy 0.4.13

Music search has clearer artist and song results, and the optional mod catalogue
now includes Track Peek.

## New

- **Peek without replacing your queue.** Track Peek adds adjacent-track hover
  cards and explicit 30-second audio previews. Inspired by Grason Chan's Spotify
  Spice, implemented natively with the existing playback engine and cache.
- **Artists have room to stand out.** Search shows a prominent top result,
  artist portraits, release shelves and compact song rows. Already loaded
  Spotify artwork can assist without additional Spotify requests or setup.

## Fixed

- **Music search excludes videos throughout.** Video recommendations and
  unclassified clips are rejected even in Songs, browse and continuation pages.
  The search field and page use a plain Search label.
- **The complete mod catalogue is easier to browse.** Responsive cards, Show all
  and included lyrics integrations make earlier mods discoverable.
- **Browse pagination continues correctly.** Artist and album pages can request
  subsequent results while preserving their current songs.
- **Unneeded visualizer components are gone.** The external preset renderer,
  settings, shortcuts, sources, translated strings and demo media were removed.
  Native fullscreen visuals, EQ and existing preferences remain supported.
