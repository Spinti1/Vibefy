Lower idle resource use and connect your phone from Playback devices.

## New

- **Nearby phones:** Vibefy Mobile 0.2.9 advertises while open. On the same Wi-Fi, select your phone in Playback devices and approve Connect on the phone. Shared playback, queue and seeking use Jam; internet access is required. Browsers can join by invitation.
- **Connected devices:** Jam participants appear in Playback devices. This is shared listening, rather than an exclusive playback transfer.

## Fixed

- **Account windows use fewer idle resources:** Spotify and YouTube account WebViews sleep between operations and close after 15 seconds of inactivity. Saved sign-in profiles remain on disk.
- **Lighter mobile/browser rendering:** disabled visual mods avoid player snapshots and layout checks. Lyrics update line styling when the active line changes.

## Thanks

Thanks to the Vibefy user for reporting resource usage. Microsoft WebView2, Android NSD, Apple Bonjour and the existing mdns-sd dependency provide the platform integrations. Physical phone/router discovery still needs device testing; no measured percentage saving is claimed.

Windows installers are unsigned.
