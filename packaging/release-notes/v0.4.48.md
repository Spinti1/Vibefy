Vibefy 0.4.48 lets you use the desktop app without connecting a music account.

## New

- **Local playlists:** create, rename and edit Vibefy playlists. Add songs from their context menu. Local changes are saved without an account.
- **Home imports:** import Spotify or YouTube playlists directly from Home. Spotify access limits and private-playlist authorization still apply. Reimporting updates the existing local copy.
- **Optional YouTube backups:** save selected playlists as unlisted copies in your connected account. Changes are reconciled with the marked copy; unmatched songs stay in the local playlist. Open a saved Vibefy playlist from the YouTube shelf to recover it on another installation.
- **File backups:** export/import playlist metadata without audio, passwords or sessions.

## Changed

- Spotify and YouTube sign-ins are optional, with Continue as guest on the welcome screen. Remove the mandatory transfer wizard and move YouTube playlist importing out of Mods. Existing local collections and linked-playlist records are retained.

The Windows installer is unsigned. The mobile web beta is separate and does not receive these desktop library changes in this release.
