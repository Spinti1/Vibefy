New Spotify playlist copies in YouTube Music are created as **Unlisted**. Anyone with the link can access them; they are not private.

- Existing private copies keep their visibility and remain linked.
- Recovery recognizes both private and unlisted copies using the same account-pair marker. Public or unknown visibility stops additions for review.
- Settings clearly explains link access before enabling sync.

The YouTube Music connection still uses the existing browser-session import. This update does not introduce Google OAuth sign-in or remove provider limits.

Credits: Spotifast by Carmine Paolino and contributors; sigma67/ytmusicapi (MIT) as the browser-session and playlist-operation protocol reference. Full credits and notices are included.
