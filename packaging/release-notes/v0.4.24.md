Spotify playlist additions can wait in the background while you keep listening.

## New
- **Background playlist additions.** Confirmed additions are saved for the signed-in account and retried after temporary Spotify rate limits. Restarting Vibefy resumes pending requests for the same account and preserves Spotify's waiting period.
- **Clear confirmation.** The app reports that an addition is queued, then confirms completion only after Spotify accepts it. Repeated pending clicks do not submit the same song again.

## Fixed
- **No blocking duplicate scan for unloaded playlists.** Loaded playlists keep their duplicate confirmation; other additions enter the background queue immediately.
- **Safe handling of uncertain writes.** Interrupted requests are not blindly replayed, avoiding duplicate songs when Spotify accepted a write but its response was lost. Authentication, permissions and exhausted quotas remain actionable errors.

Vibefy must be running for pending requests to progress. Spotify application access requirements and quotas still apply; completion time is not guaranteed.
