Available synchronized lyrics now take priority over plain text from another provider.

## Fixed
- **Keep looking for synchronized lyrics.** Plain Spicy Lyrics and Spotify results no longer stop the fallback search. A compatible timed LRCLIB result or timed More Lyrics result can replace plain text, preserving the chosen source's attribution.
- **Check other LRCLIB entries.** An exact album entry with text only no longer hides a compatible synchronized entry. Plain text is kept when alternatives fail or have no timing. Existing artist and duration checks remain in place for upgrades.
- **Refresh incomplete answers sooner.** Plain LRCLIB results and misses are retried after six hours instead of thirty days. Existing timed results keep their longer cache.

Songs with no available timestamps remain plain text. Line synchronization does not imply word-by-word timing. Local recording alignment and its resource usage are unchanged.
