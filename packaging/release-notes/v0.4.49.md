Vibefy 0.4.49 removes the retired Linked playlist system.

## Changed

- **Simpler library:** remove the Linked shelf and its transfer service, review state and dual-platform mutation queues. Spotify and YouTube playlist actions now affect the chosen platform independently.
- **Keep existing playlists:** recover old local snapshots once in the background. Completed copies appear as ordinary YouTube playlists; unfinished copies remain local. Original recovery files are retained, deleted entries are excluded and newer local edits are kept.
- **Less background work:** no old transfer task or account-pair observation. Explicit YouTube backups for Vibefy playlists remain available.

The Windows installer is unsigned. The mobile web beta is separate.
