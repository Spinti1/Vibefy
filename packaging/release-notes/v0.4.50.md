Vibefy 0.4.50 simplifies the library and includes the desktop updates since 0.4.42.

## New

- **Optional accounts:** connect Spotify or YouTube, or continue as a guest.
- **Vibefy playlists:** create local playlists, import Spotify playlists and optionally save unlisted YouTube backups. Metadata can also be exported to a file. Connected YouTube playlists open from their library shelf.
- **Built-in visualizers:** 16 effects based on Vivy, Backmusic and SoundVisualizer, with centered artwork and colours from the album cover.
- **Resume your session:** remember the last song, position, playlist and queue. Playback resumes when you press Play.
- **Cloud Jam:** share a room through a link or code without direct connections between participants. Mobile browser restrictions still apply.

## Fixed

- Continue past unavailable recordings, with a limit on consecutive failures. Connection errors and rate limits remain visible.
- Prefer identifiable explicit recordings over labelled clean/censored versions. Unlabelled censorship cannot always be detected.
- Remove the retired Linked shelf and transfer service. Existing local playlist snapshots are recovered without deleting remote playlists. Spotify and YouTube actions now operate independently.
- Remove the redundant YouTube import button from Home. Add development-artifact cleanup; large compilation folders are not part of the installed app.
- Update CI to use the retained playlist helper tests after removal of the old transfer service.
