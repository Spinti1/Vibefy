Playback recovery for unavailable recordings.

## Fixed

- **Playlist playback continues** past a missing, restricted or unmatched recording instead of stopping at the first failure. Three consecutive failures stop playback to avoid repeatedly skipping through the queue.
- **Connection errors are clearer.** Source timeouts and rate limits are distinguished from unavailable recordings and do not automatically skip songs.
- **Shutdown cancels pending playback preparation**, preventing an old download from starting playback after the engine closes.

Unavailable recordings remain unavailable; this update does not bypass regional or protected-content restrictions.
