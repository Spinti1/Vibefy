Vibefy 0.4.10 gives WaveLink its own search and reduces repeated work while searching and starting music.

## New

- **SoundCloud search sits beside the main search after installing WaveLink.** Open its independent query/results page, search with Enter, play public uploads or save them on Home. Narrow windows keep both searches together on a second toolbar row.

## Fixed

- **Recent searches return from a small session cache.** Complete successful results remain for two minutes; typing starts a request after 180 ms. Search and selected-track metadata retain request capacity while library work is busy.
- **Starting music avoids a repeated watch-page lookup.** Verified recordings reuse their inspected audio metadata with the original download path as a fallback. Play and prefetch share in-flight preparation; skipped-track work is cancelled and prefetch avoids whole-file cache reads.
