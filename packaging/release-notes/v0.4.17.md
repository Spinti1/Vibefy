YouTube songs can now be matched to a Spotify recording and added to an editable Spotify playlist. The website also shows a clean desktop demo and clearer project credits.

## New
- **Add source songs to Spotify playlists.** Right-click a song in Search or an imported playlist, choose “Add to Spotify playlist…”, check the suggested recording, and choose your playlist. Suggestions rank title, artist, duration and version; nothing is added until you select the recording and destination.
- **Edit the match query.** Shorten a video title or adjust the artist when the initial search does not find the right recording. Spotify availability and account permissions still apply.

## Fixed
- **Source identifiers stay out of Spotify writes.** YouTube IDs are never submitted as Spotify track URIs. Existing duplicate checks and playlist permission rules are preserved. Closed or superseded match dialogs ignore late search responses.
- **More direct website information.** The home page uses the actual desktop app with sample playlists, factual feature descriptions and visible links to source projects. The mini player is explicitly credited to Spotifast with Vibefy's visual redesign.
