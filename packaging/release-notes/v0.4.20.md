Less repeated work while displaying lyrics and visualizers, with release packaging tuned for size.

## Fixed
- **Shared lyrics data.** Rendering and manual sync settings share the loaded lyrics instead of copying the complete document each frame. Track changes still reject stale provider responses.
- **Reused visualizer buffers.** Current bands and Vivy's rolling history reuse their allocations after warm-up. Backmusic does not build an unused history.
- **Release packaging.** Full link-time optimization retains speed optimizations, and Setup uses stronger compression. All playback helpers, Auto Sync models and notices remain included. Unused constants were removed without changing playback rules.
