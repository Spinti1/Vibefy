Vibefy 0.4.46 fixes artwork and colours in the built-in visualizers.

- Album covers and the Album cover setting are available in all 16 effects.
- Use source artwork or a smaller cover when large artwork is missing; keep the thumbnail visible while the large copy loads.
- Add multi-colour gradients to lines, frequency bars and particles, retaining the brightness control.
- No changes to playback or account connections. Original visualizer credits remain included.

The Windows installer is unsigned.
