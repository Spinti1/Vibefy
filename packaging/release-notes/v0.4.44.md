Vibefy 0.4.44 adds shared cloud Jam sessions and optional SoundVisualizer effects, and improves audio edition selection and playlist deletion.

### New

- **Listen Together across desktop and mobile web.** Host or join through a private link/code using the Cloudflare relay. Participants do not connect to each other. Includes queue suggestions, periodic timing correction and bounded reconnection. Up to 24 listeners per six-hour session; no audio or music-account credentials are relayed.
- **12 optional SoundVisualizer 2D effects.** Adapted from CaYatur / Çağan Turgut / CaYaDev under MIT, available as a separate installable mod. GPU/3D, Canvas bloom and MilkDrop modes are not included.
- **Linked playlist deletion.** Confirm once to remove the Spotify library entry and delete its identified YouTube copy. Each acknowledgement is saved and retried independently. Owned YouTube playlists also have a delete action.

### Fixed

- **Audio editions:** clean/censored/radio-edit labels in titles, credits and albums are rejected for ordinary recordings; explicit labels are preferred among identified versions. Metadata cannot reliably identify unlabelled censorship.
- **Fewer playback lookups:** confirmed official audio can start before remaining fallback queries, and queued recordings keep their known metadata.
- **Jam button:** fixed access from the top bar, independent of player-bar space.
- **Visualizers:** real PCM for waveform effects, bounded 30 Hz history, separate per-mod settings, clipping and sandbox limits.
- Includes the unavailable-recording recovery from 0.4.43.

### Thanks

Spotifast by Carmine Paolino and contributors provides the native foundation. SoundVisualizer by CaYatur provides the credited 2D geometry/audio references. Full attributions and original notices are in CREDITS.md and each mod package.

Windows installers are unsigned. Service quotas, recording availability and mobile browser playback restrictions still apply. Spotify exposes library removal rather than permanent playlist erasure. No real user playlist was deleted during validation.
