# Vibefy recording selection

These rules describe the native Spotify-to-YouTube resolver in `src/player.rs`, `src/recording_match.rs`, `src/title_similarity.rs` and `src/audio_match.rs`. They do not describe the separate Spotify recording picker used for playlist insertion. Version 0.4.19 relaxes title equality into bounded similarity.

1. **Reference metadata.** Playback waits for a nonempty track title and a named artist. The reference includes all Spotify artists, album and duration. A cached match is reused only with a supported matcher version, source information, the same reference key and a compatible duration.
2. **Search and inspection.** Queries combine the primary artist/title, all artists with “official audio”, “topic”, and the album when useful. Each query requests up to 20 results. Responses and inspections are processed as they arrive; a qualifying fully inspected catalogue recording does not wait for a slower sibling request. Cheap checks create a ranked shortlist of at most 12; full watch-page metadata is inspected before an uncached recording is authorized. An inspected distributed recording within three seconds can finish early. Failed downloads try other accepted candidates.
3. **Title similarity.** Case, punctuation, common accents, identifiable artist prefixes and presentation tags such as “Official Audio” are ignored for comparison. Exact titles rank above similar ones. Spacing changes, reordered words and small spelling differences can qualify: the edit allowance is 15% of the longer compact title, rounded down, with a minimum of one and maximum of three edits. Approximate spelling requires at least eight characters on both sides; nonexact comparisons are bounded to 256 characters. Numbers and trailing Roman-numeral editions must agree. Short names and a shared word alone are insufficient. Structured music-track credits use the same comparison and cannot contradict the chosen title.
4. **Artist identity.** The primary artist must be identifiable through structured music credits, an artist-like channel name, title credits, or specific description credits. Conflicting structured artist metadata rejects the result. Additional artists affect ranking. A channel called “official” alone is not proof of authenticity.
5. **Version and duration.** Unrequested live/acoustic/remix/cover/karaoke/instrumental/sped/slowed and other altered variants are rejected. Extra checks reject tags such as bass boosted, 8D, mashup, TikTok edit, snippets, loops, previews and an unrequested clean version. Active/upcoming livestreams are rejected. Normally duration must differ by no more than 15 seconds. For a verified-channel official video of a song at least 90 seconds long, a longer intro/outro may be accepted up to the smaller of 45 seconds and one quarter of the song duration. Missing or nonpositive duration in the inspected candidate is rejected; an unknown reference duration cannot establish a timing difference.
6. **Ranking and audio.** Distributed catalogue audio receives the largest source bonus, followed by evidence for verified artists, verified publishers, credited publishers and artist channels. Duration closeness, matching album and collaborator credits also affect the score; official videos receive a small penalty. An independent upload can still qualify as a metadata match and is not labelled official. The native audio selector uses the best available M4A audio, with compatible AAC in combined MP4 as fallback. Supported ordinary combined MP4 files are compacted to audio-only after a two-second background delay, one at a time, without recoding audio samples or changing the original audio timing/edit tables. The playing buffer is kept intact. The cache is replaced atomically only if its source file is unchanged; unsupported, fragmented or malformed layouts are retained. Existing files are handled on access. No extra executable is shipped. There is no hard bitrate minimum.

This is metadata matching, not audio fingerprinting against the Spotify master. It cannot prove identical mastering, exclude every misleading upload, or guarantee lyric timing. Auto Sync aligns lyrics to the recording that actually plays; it is a separate stage. Direct YouTube/SoundCloud songs retain their chosen source identity and do not pass through Spotify-to-YouTube matching.
