Google verification pending

Google may show “Google hasn’t verified this app”. Vibefy’s YouTube integration is public, but has not completed Google’s review.

You no longer need a tester invitation. Until Google approves the YouTube permissions, a 100-user cap and the unverified-app warning still apply. You do not need your own Google Cloud project or Client ID.

Sign-in happens on Google. Vibefy does not receive your password.

Permissions & privacy
  • Your password stays with Google. Vibefy receives a temporary access token, kept in this tab’s memory and excluded from playlist backups.
  • Connecting requests read access to your YouTube account to load your playlists, plus basic profile information for your account name.
  • Saving or editing a YouTube copy asks separately for broader YouTube management access. Google’s permission also covers editing and permanently deleting YouTube content. Vibefy uses it for playlist changes.
  • Review Google’s permission screen before deciding. You can disconnect in Vibefy or revoke access in your Google Account. Public availability does not mean Google has verified the app.
