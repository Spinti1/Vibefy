Listen Together

Host a Jam and invite other Vibefy web players. Everyone plays the same recording on their own device.

No active session.

Direct encrypted WebRTC connection, up to 8 listeners. Uses PeerJS Cloud for signalling and Google STUN to establish the connection. Share invitations privately. Some networks require the same Wi-Fi or a VPN. Web Jams and desktop LAN sessions currently use different protocols.

Keep Now Playing open for official YouTube playback. A participant may need to press Play once. Timing is corrected periodically; it is not sample-accurate.
