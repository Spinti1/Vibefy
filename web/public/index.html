Vibefy is an independent fork of Spotifast by Carmine Paolino, which provides the native interface and player foundation. Thank you to all original contributors.

Core: Rust, egui / eframe, fastframe, librespot, Symphonia, yt-dlp, whisper.cpp / ggml and OpenAI Whisper. Search reference: sigma67/ytmusicapi. Lyrics: Spikerko/Spicy Lyrics, Kamiloo13/More Lyrics, LRCLIB and their credited providers.

Native adaptations and inspiration: NoahClemmensen/Nyan Bar Synced, kyrie25 and adryd325/Oneko, BlafKing/Cat Jam Synced, xoft122/WaveLink, wairence/Vivy, rolandyangg/Backmusic, Grason Chan/Spotify Spice (Track Peek only), Kyzenkms/Spicetify Jam (independent LAN implementation).
